# Sound Painter v1.1: new chapters and enhancements (scope locked 2026-11-04)

Owner-approved in chat on 2026-11-04. Everything follows the v1 rules: errorless (no failing, no timers, no scores), any sound makes progress, a character models first, parent-chosen level, calm mode respected, mic only on listening screens, nothing recorded.

## Build order (one at a time, each verified before the next)

| # | Piece | What the child does | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Phonics Safari: 3 pictures per vowel** | 15 pictures on 5 vowel islands (a: apple, ant, alligator · e: egg, elephant, elf · i: igloo, insect, ink · o: octopus, ostrich, ox · u: umbrella, up, under); a star per picture, an island badge when all 3 are done | Reuses vowel recognition and the colour-in screen. Practise level still speeds up on the matching vowel |
| 2 | **Animal Sounds** (new world, Aria) | Copy moo, baa, meow, woof, quack, hiss, roar to wake each sleeping animal | Any voice wakes the animal. A close match wakes it faster: vowel-like sounds use the vowel recogniser; "hiss" uses unpitched noise detection |
| 3 | **Pip's Ice Hill** (pitch) | Sing higher or lower to slide Pip up and down the hill and collect stars at different heights | Stars are placed within the child's measured range, so a narrow range still reaches all of them |
| 4 | **Ollie's Lanterns** (loudness) | Ollie asks for a whisper or a big voice; quiet sounds light small lanterns and loud sounds light big ones | Both kinds of sound light something; no wrong answer |
| 5 | **Breath Balloon** (hold a sound) | Hold one sound to inflate a balloon until it floats away | Builds sustained voicing and breath; the length needed adapts to the child |
| 6 | **Stickers + today's path** | Every finished lesson earns a sticker in a book; Home shows a short "today's path" for a predictable routine | No streak pressure: missing a day loses nothing |
| 7 | **Dashboard per chapter** | Grown-ups see practice per chapter: pictures, animals, held-sound length, pitch range reached on the hill, quiet/loud lanterns lit | On-device only, as in v1 |
