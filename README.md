# Sound Painter: Color My Voice

Children paint with their voice: loudness sets the brush size, pitch sets the colour, and held vowel sounds colour in pictures.

It's built for children aged 3–6 who learn at their own pace. There are no failure states, and every sound makes art.

- Free, with no ads, tracking or accounts.
- The microphone is processed on the device in real time. Audio is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.
- Android, built with Flutter.

See `docs/` for the design document and the engineering proposal.
