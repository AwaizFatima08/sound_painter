Sound Painter

Children paint with their voice. Sing, hum or say “aaah” and glowing colour flows across the screen.

Coming soon to Google Play · Free

Three gentle ways to play

Free Canvas

Loud sounds make big strokes, high sounds paint warm reds, low sounds cool purples. Little fingers can paint too.

Phonics Safari

Aria the turtle models a vowel sound. Hold it, and the apple, egg, igloo, octopus or umbrella colours itself in.

Pip’s warm-up

A one-minute game that learns each child’s own voice range, so even a narrow range paints the whole rainbow.

Made for children aged 3–6 who learn at their own pace

  • No failing, no timers, no scores. Every sound makes art.
  • Friendly spoken prompts, so no reading is needed.
  • Explore and Practise levels, chosen by a grown-up, not by age.
  • Calm mode with fewer sparkles and softer motion.
  • An optional session time limit that ends with a gentle goodbye.
  • A simple grown-ups’ dashboard: play time, voice time, vowels practised and voice range.

Private by design

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no tracking. The app has no internet access: the microphone is analysed on the device in real time, and nothing is ever recorded or sent anywhere. Read the privacy policy.
